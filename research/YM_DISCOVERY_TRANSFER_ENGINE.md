### DTE-01: AI feature discovery, adversarial repair, human proof

[Davies et al., Nature 600 (2021), 70–74](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04086-x), topology section and Fig. 3: learned dependence between geometric knot invariants and signature suggested a relationship. Counterexamples to an initial conjecture forced additional geometric information into the formulation. The paper distinguishes the proved knot result from a representation-theory conjecture. This supplies a discovery protocol: attribution -> reduced variables -> adversarial examples -> repaired statement -> proof. It does not supply a YM inequality.

YM hypothesis: a proposed insertion norm depending only on polymer size misses flow-radius or boundary geometry. Test independent variables such as source support, coarse-scale radius sqrt(t), separation, local field sector and RG depth. A finite collection of sampled configurations cannot establish uniformity. Falsification must target concentrated curvature, disconnected supports and growing RG depth, not only typical Monte Carlo samples. Transfer object: XFER-YM-DTE-0001.

### DTE-02: program search with an independent evaluator

[Romera-Paredes et al., Nature 625 (2024), 468–475; published 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06924-6): FunSearch searches programs through generation and evaluation, producing cap-set constructions and bin-packing heuristics. Verifiable finite constructions and general algorithms have different proof obligations. Mechanism: restrict proposal space, use an evaluator external to the generator, retain diverse branches, and inspect interpretable programs.

YM hypothesis: search a finite family of weighted polymer norms or correction maps with rational coefficients. Evaluate algebraic identities and finite incompatibility inequalities exactly. Learned weights remain candidates until the infinite polymer family and every cutoff are controlled analytically. A sampled inequality or solver status is not a certificate. Transfer object: XFER-YM-DTE-0002.

### DTE-03: dual constraints guide an exact analytic representation

[Cohn–Elkies, Annals 157 (2003), 689–714](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0110009), principal theorems and sharpness conditions; [Viazovska, Annals 185 (2017), 991–1015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04246): a Fourier-based upper-bound framework and a special-function construction prove optimality of E8 packing. The reusable mechanism is to identify the certificate's sign, transform and equality requirements first, then choose a representation capable of satisfying all of them. The modular-form construction is dimension-specific; neither it nor the sphere-packing theorem directly applies to gauge fields.

YM hypothesis: derive a dual majorant of source derivatives or a positive quadratic form before optimizing a flowed-observable bound. Equality and normalization conditions should expose omitted terms. A YM certificate needs its own admissibility theorem; sphere-packing positivity is not OS reflection positivity. Transfer object: XFER-YM-DTE-0003.

### DTE-04: blueprint plus machine proof plus semantic audit

[Liquid Tensor Experiment blueprint, introduction](https://leanprover-community.github.io/liquid/sect0001.html), and [maintainers' completion account](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/lte-final/): the project decomposes a research theorem into named dependencies connected to Lean declarations. Check intended SU(N) measure, dimension four, physical normalization, fixed flow time, finite volume, cutoff quantifier, and every limit order separately. Transfer object: XFER-YM-DTE-0004.

### DTE-05: abstract polymer criteria as a neighboring-model mechanism

[Kotecky–Preiss, CMP 103 (1986), 491–498](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01211762) gives a convergence criterion for abstract polymer models. Mechanism: separate model-specific representation and activity estimates from model-independent convergence. This is closer to YM-015F than the discovery examples above, but identifying a YM RG representation with the required source-dependent hard-core polymer model is itself missing work.

Proposed sufficient criterion, to be matched to the exact source convention: for analytic activities w_(a,r)(gamma;z), nonnegative size A(gamma), and source polydisc of radii rho_j,

`sup_z sum_(gamma' incompatible gamma) |w_(a,r)(gamma';z)| exp(A(gamma')) <= A(gamma)`.

It must hold uniformly in a and RG scale r, with controlled normalization and contributions beyond polymer activities. The abstract theorem alone does not provide this. Transfer object: XFER-YM-DTE-0005.

Repeated sources require factorials according to derivative multiplicities. Normalize tests with ||f_j||_H3 <= 1 and recover general tests by multilinearity. This is a conditional proposed bridge; no such uniform polydisc is proved here. Control of log Z gives cumulants first; moments additionally require the finite partition relation and the deterministic/source-dependent prefactor. Do not omit it.

An infinite-volume log Z bound generally scales with volume. The present target fixes physical volume but the number of lattice sites diverges. A bound proportional to lattice volume is insufficient. Likewise, each O_a is bounded at each cutoff, so its moment generating function is entire there; this supplies no uniform M_k or source radius control.
