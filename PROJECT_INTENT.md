# PROJECT INTENT — YANG-MILLS AND THE MASS GAP

## 1. Project identity

- Project name: Yang–Mills and the Mass Gap

- Intended users/stakeholders: Human project owner, mathematical researchers, independent verifiers, and future maintainers of the research record.
