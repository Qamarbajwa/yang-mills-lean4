## Executive Summary
Bałaban developed gauge-invariant block-spin averaging transformations and axial/Coulomb gauge-fixing operators on hypercubic lattices, establishing rigorous decay bounds and regularity for lattice gauge propagators under scale transformations.

## Key Proven Results
- Definition of gauge-covariant block-spin averaging operators for compact Lie groups on multi-scale lattices.
- Covariant gauge-fixing on lattice blocks with controlled ghost and Faddeev-Popov determinants.
- Uniform exponential decay and smoothness bounds on gauge propagators across renormalization group steps.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Technical foundational machinery for multi-scale RG; does not by itself establish the full partition function bound.
- Operates on finite volume with UV cutoff a > 0.
