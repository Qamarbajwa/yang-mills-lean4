## Executive Summary
Markus Göpfert and Gerhard Mack established a rigorous mathematical proof of confinement of static quarks in 3D pure U(1) lattice gauge theory with Villain action for all values of the coupling constant. They proved non-zero string tension sigma > 0 for all g^2, constructed the continuum limit with Debye mass m_D fixed, and showed it converges to a free massive scalar field.

## Key Proven Results
- Proof of non-zero string tension sigma > 0 for all coupling constant values in 3D U(1) Villain lattice gauge theory.
- Rigorous mathematical justification of Polyakov's 1977 semiclassical monopole Debye screening mechanism on a discrete lattice.
- Controlled continuum limit with fixed physical Debye mass m_D > 0 yielding a massive scalar field.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Applies strictly to 2+1 dimensions (3D Euclidean spacetime).
- Applies to Abelian U(1) gauge group; does not prove non-Abelian confinement in 4D.
- Continuum limit with fixed m_D yields a free massive scalar field rather than interacting 4D Yang-Mills.
