## Executive Summary
Paul Federbush introduced phase-cell cluster expansions and master inequalities for constructive Euclidean Yang-Mills theory, providing an alternative path to local gauge field stability and multi-scale cluster expansions.

## Key Proven Results
- Phase-cell cluster expansion formalism for Yang-Mills lattice gauge theories.
- Master inequalities bounding non-Abelian link fluctuations and plaquette interactions across block-spin scales.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Provides UV estimates in bounded volumes; does not solve the 4D infinite-volume infrared mass gap problem.
