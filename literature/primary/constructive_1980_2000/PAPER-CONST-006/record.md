## Executive Summary
Bałaban completed the proof of ultraviolet stability for 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills theory in a finite volume. By combining small-field effective actions with polymer expansions for large-field deviations, he proved uniform bounds on the partition function as the lattice spacing a -> 0.

## Key Proven Results
- Rigorous proof of ultraviolet stability (UV convergence as a -> 0) for 4D pure Yang-Mills in a finite box.
- Convergent polymer expansions controlling large-field deviations with exponentially small statistical weights.
- Complete mathematical control of the continuum UV limit in a fixed volume.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Constructed in a finite volume V < infty with an infrared boundary / cutoff.
- Does NOT construct the infinite-volume thermodynamic limit V -> infty with a non-zero mass gap Delta > 0 in 4D continuum.
- The infrared problem (long-distance confinement and mass gap generation) remained unsolved.
