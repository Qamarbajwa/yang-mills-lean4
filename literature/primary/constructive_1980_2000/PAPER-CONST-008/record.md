## Executive Summary
Magnen, Rivasseau, and Sénéor achieved the rigorous constructive mathematical construction of 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills theory in the presence of an infrared cutoff (finite volume or mass regulator), proving UV Borel summability and constructive existence of Schwinger distributions with IR regularization.

## Key Proven Results
- Rigorous construction of 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills Schwinger functions with an artificial infrared cutoff.
- Multi-scale cluster expansion combining phase-space analysis with Slavnov-Taylor gauge Ward identities.
- Constructive proof that the ultraviolet limit a -> 0 exists mathematically in 4D.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Requires an explicit infrared cutoff (infrared mass regulator or finite volume).
- Removal of the infrared cutoff (IR limit) is obstructed by infrared renormalons and the unproved mass gap Delta > 0 in 4D.
- Does not satisfy the Clay Millennium requirements because the infinite-volume limit with Delta > 0 is not constructed.
