## Executive Summary
Bałaban formulated the small-field inductive renormalization group program for 4D lattice gauge theories, proving that inductive scale integration generates an effective action dominated by asymptotically free running couplings and controlled local counterterms.

## Key Proven Results
- Inductive step theorem for small-field effective actions in 4D SU(N) lattice gauge theory.
- Rigorous non-perturbative verification of asymptotic freedom running in the small-field region.
- Uniform stability of the quadratic action and local counterterm flow across an arbitrary number of UV scale steps.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Restricted to small-field configurations where gauge field fluctuations satisfy |F_P| < epsilon.
- Requires large-field polymer expansion to control non-perturbative fluctuations.
- Operates in finite volume with infrared cutoff.
