## Executive Summary
Brydges, Fröhlich, and Spencer proved rigorous mathematical bounds for 3D classical Coulomb gases, establishing exponential decay of correlations (Debye screening) and mapping to dipole gases via convergent sine-Gordon polymer expansions.

## Key Proven Results
- Rigorous proof of exponential clustering and Debye screening in 3D Coulomb systems.
- Convergent polymer and cluster expansions for sine-Gordon / monopole gas representations.
- Mathematical foundation for rigorous mass generation in 3D gauge-related statistical systems.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Restricted to 3D statistical mechanics / Abelian Coulomb representations.
- Does not construct non-Abelian gauge fields in 4 dimensions.
