## Executive Summary
Erhard Seiler synthesized the rigorous foundations of lattice gauge theories, Osterwalder-Schrader reflection positivity on lattices, physical Hilbert space construction via transfer matrices, high-temperature / strong-coupling polymer expansions, and diamagnetic inequalities.

## Key Proven Results
- Comprehensive axiomatic formulation of lattice gauge theories satisfying OS reflection positivity.
- Rigorous transfer matrix spectral analysis on hypercubic lattices for general compact Lie groups.
- Mathematical proof of string tension bounds and mass gap estimates in strong-coupling regimes.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Mass gap and confinement bounds require fixed non-zero lattice cutoff a > 0 or strong coupling g^2 >> 1.
- Continuum limit a -> 0 at weak coupling g -> 0 remains unconstructed in 4D.
