## Executive Summary
Cao, Nissim, and Sheffield developed a dynamical approach building on Shen-Zhu-Zhu functional inequalities, deriving area law bounds from a spectral mass gap condition in 't Hooft coupling regimes for gauge groups U(N), SU(N), and SO(2N).

## Key Proven Results
- Formulation of dynamical approach linking lattice spectral gap condition to Wilson area law.
- Rigorous area law bounds in 't Hooft parameter regimes for U(N), SU(N), and SO(2N) lattice gauge theories.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Operates in 't Hooft regimes on discrete lattices.
- Lattice area law from dynamical gap conditions does not establish the Clay 4D continuum mass gap.
