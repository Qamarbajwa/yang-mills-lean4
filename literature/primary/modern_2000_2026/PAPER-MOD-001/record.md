## Executive Summary
Chandra, Chevyrev, Hairer, and Shen established a rigorous mathematical construction of singular stochastic partial differential equations (SPDEs) for non-Abelian Yang-Mills and Yang-Mills-Higgs fields in 2 and 3 dimensions. Using regularity structures and gauge-covariant mollification, they constructed the state space of gauge orbits and proved that the renormalized Langevin dynamic generates a canonical Markov process leaving the Euclidean Yang-Mills-Higgs measure invariant.

## Key Proven Results
- Rigorous local existence and renormalization of singular stochastic Yang-Mills-Higgs flow in 2D and 3D.
- Construction of gauge-orbit metric space and gauge-invariant Markov diffusion process.
- Proof of invariance of the Euclidean Yang-Mills measure under stochastic quantization in 2D and 3D.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Constructed in 2 and 3 spacetime dimensions; 4D Yang-Mills SPDE is critically singular and remains mathematically unconstructed.
- Does not prove a mass gap in 4D continuum spacetime.
- Infrared infinite-volume measure construction in 3D/4D requires separate non-perturbative bounds.
