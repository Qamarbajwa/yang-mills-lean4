# Clay Mathematics Institute: Yang-Mills & The Mass Gap (Current Status)

Source URL: https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/
Status: Unsolved

## Summary

Quantum Yang-Mills theory is the mathematical framework behind the Standard Model of particle physics. The Millennium Prize Problem asks to prove that for any compact simple gauge group G, a non-trivial quantum Yang-Mills theory exists on R^4 and has a mass gap Delta > 0.

## Primary Documents

- Official problem description: Arthur Jaffe and Edward Witten, *Quantum Yang-Mills Theory* (yangmills.pdf)
