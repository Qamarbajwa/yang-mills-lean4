## Executive Summary
Witten (1998) constructed a holographic model of 4D pure Yang-Mills by compactifying 5D AdS black hole geometry on a thermal circle with SUSY-breaking boundary conditions, proving that classical supergravity on the capped geometry exhibits a discrete glueball spectrum with mass gap Delta > 0 and Wilson loop area law. Polchinski & Strassler (2001) modeled hard scattering via warped geometry.

## Key Proven Results
- Derivation of discrete glueball mass spectrum and area law from higher-dimensional classical gravity on warped backgrounds.
- Geometric realization of confinement and Hawking-Page confinement/deconfinement thermal phase transition.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Holographic physics argument relying on unproved AdS/CFT duality and large-N, large-lambda supergravity limit.
- Does not constitute a mathematical proof or constructive QFT of 4D pure Yang-Mills.
