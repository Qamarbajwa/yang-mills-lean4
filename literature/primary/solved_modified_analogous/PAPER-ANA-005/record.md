## Executive Summary
Seiberg and Witten (1994) determined the exact low-energy effective action of 4D N=2 SU(2) SYM in terms of an elliptic modular curve. At singular points on moduli space, monopoles/dyons become massless; adding a small N=1 mass deformation triggers monopole condensation and rigorous dual Meissner confinement with a mass gap.

## Key Proven Results
- Exact non-perturbative determination of low-energy Wilsonian effective action for 4D N=2 SU(2) gauge theory.
- Physical proof of confinement via condensation of magnetically charged BPS states upon N=1 perturbation.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Exact physics result relying on supersymmetric holomorphy and modular consistency; not a constructive QFT existence theorem.
- Does not apply to pure non-supersymmetric 4D Yang-Mills.
