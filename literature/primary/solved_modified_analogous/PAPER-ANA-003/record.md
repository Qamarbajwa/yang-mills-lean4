- **MASS_OR_GAP_MECHANISM:** 2D chiral anomaly / fermion vacuum polarization generating dynamical photon mass and exact bosonization
- **TRANSFER_BARRIER:** Mechanism is strictly dependent on 2D spacetime (where [e] has mass dimension 1), Abelian U(1) gauge symmetry, fundamental dynamical Dirac fermions, and exact 1+1D bosonization. Pure 4D YM has no fundamental fermions, d=4, non-Abelian self-interactions, and no exact 4D bosonization.
- **TRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Demonstration that local gauge invariance does not forbid a non-zero gauge-boson mass when vacuum polarization develops an infrared pole (Schwinger mechanism).
- **NONTRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Exact 1+1D fermion-boson duality (bosonization), linear 2D Coulomb potential V(r) ~ |r|, and reliance on fundamental dynamical Dirac fermions.

## Executive Summary
Schwinger (1962) solved 1+1D massless QED (Schwinger model), showing that fermion vacuum polarization dynamically generates a non-zero photon mass m_gamma^2 = e^2 / pi without breaking gauge invariance. Lowenstein & Swieca (1971) established the rigorous operator and Wightman reconstruction.

## Key Proven Results
- Exact proof of dynamical gauge-boson mass generation via fermion loop vacuum polarization in 1+1D.
- Complete screening of fractional charge and confinement of fermions into neutral massive bosons.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Restricted to 1+1D Abelian gauge theory with fundamental fermions.
- Does not explain pure 4D non-Abelian Yang-Mills mass gap in the absence of fundamental fermions.
