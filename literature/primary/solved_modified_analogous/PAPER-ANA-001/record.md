## Executive Summary
Driver (1989), Sengupta (1993, 1997), and Lévy (2010) constructed rigorous, non-perturbative probability measures for pure Yang-Mills theory on compact 2D Riemannian manifolds using Brownian motion and heat kernels on compact Lie groups, establishing an exact area-dependent Wilson loop calculus and continuous Euclidean Schwinger functions.

## Key Proven Results
- Rigorous construction of gauge-invariant probability measures for 2D Yang-Mills on arbitrary compact 2D surfaces.
- Exact calculation of Wilson loop expectation values obeying an explicit area-law decay proportional to the Casimir invariant.
- Verification of reflection positivity and Osterwalder-Schrader reconstruction for 2D cylinder/torus geometries.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Strictly 2-dimensional; pure 2D Yang-Mills has zero local propagating degrees of freedom.
- Does not encounter or resolve the 4D ultraviolet renormalization group problem or 4D transverse gluon dynamics.
