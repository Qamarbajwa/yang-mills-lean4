- **MASS_OR_GAP_MECHANISM:** Large-N steepest-descent / unitary matrix eigenvalue saddle point on 2D lattice
- **TRANSFER_BARRIER:** Exact saddle-point solution requires N -> infinity on a discrete 2D single-plaquette lattice, suppressing 1/N non-planar fluctuations, higher-dimensional loop entanglement, and the non-trivial 4D continuum scaling trajectory.
- **TRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Identification of large-N master fields, eigenvalue density techniques, and the concept of third-order phase transitions separating weak and strong coupling regimes.
- **NONTRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Planar single-plaquette matrix factorization, suppression of multi-plaquette correlations, and reliance on N -> infinity without 4D continuum renormalization.

## Executive Summary
Gross & Witten (1980) and Wadia (1980) solved 2D U(N) lattice gauge theory in the large-N limit via steepest descent, discovering a 3rd-order phase transition at 't Hooft coupling lambda_c = g^2 N = 2 where the unitary eigenvalue distribution develops a gap.

## Key Proven Results
- Exact analytic evaluation of the 2D lattice partition function and free energy in the large-N limit.
- Discovery of the Gross-Witten-Wadia third-order phase transition separating strong-coupling and weak-coupling eigenvalue regimes.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Exact physics solution relying on large-N saddle-point method; not a rigorous probabilistic construction of 4D continuum measures.
- 2D single-plaquette lattice model lacks 4D dynamical propagating gluons.
