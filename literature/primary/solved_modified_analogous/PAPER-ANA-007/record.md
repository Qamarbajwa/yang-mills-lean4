## Executive Summary
Mandelstam (1983) and Brink, Schwarz, & Scherk (1977) established all-order perturbative finiteness results and the associated superconformal structure of 4D N=4 Super Yang-Mills. These are exact results in protected or specified sectors of the N=4 superconformal QFT, not a claim that the complete finite-N nonperturbative QFT problem is solved in the Clay sense.

## Key Proven Results
- All-orders proof of ultraviolet finiteness and vanishing beta function beta(g) = 0 in N=4 SYM.
- Establishment of exact superconformal/protected-sector structure and the associated S-duality framework.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- N=4 SYM has no mass gap (Delta = 0, scale-invariant continuous spectrum).
- Cannot serve as a mass-gap model for pure Yang-Mills; demonstrates that maximal SUSY eliminates the mass gap.
