## Executive Summary
Higgs (1964) and Kibble (1967) formulated gauge-boson mass generation via scalar vacuum expectation values. Fradkin & Shenker (1979) proved a theorem for lattice gauge-Higgs models: in the relevant fundamental-Higgs parameter region, Higgs and confinement regimes can be connected by an analytic path without a thermodynamic phase boundary. This analyticity result is distinct from the Higgs mass mechanism and is not a complete solution of pure Yang-Mills.

## Key Proven Results
- Classical mechanism of gauge boson mass generation via scalar spontaneous symmetry breaking.
- Rigorous mathematical proof (Fradkin-Shenker 1979) of analyticity along the relevant path and the absence of a separating thermodynamic phase boundary in the stated lattice gauge-Higgs regime.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Mass generation depends explicitly on fundamental scalar fields with non-zero vacuum expectation value.
- Does not describe dynamical mass generation in pure Yang-Mills theory without scalar fields.
