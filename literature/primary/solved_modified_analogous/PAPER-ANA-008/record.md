## Executive Summary
Higgs (1964) and Kibble (1967) formulated classical gauge-boson mass generation via scalar vacuum expectation values. Fradkin & Shenker (1979) proved a rigorous mathematical theorem on lattice gauge-Higgs models showing that the Higgs and confinement regimes are smoothly connected by an analytic path in the phase diagram.

## Key Proven Results
- Classical mechanism of gauge boson mass generation via scalar spontaneous symmetry breaking.
- Rigorous mathematical proof (Fradkin-Shenker 1979) of analyticity and absence of phase boundaries between Higgs and confinement regimes with fundamental scalars.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Mass generation depends explicitly on fundamental scalar fields with non-zero vacuum expectation value.
- Does not describe dynamical mass generation in pure Yang-Mills theory without scalar fields.
