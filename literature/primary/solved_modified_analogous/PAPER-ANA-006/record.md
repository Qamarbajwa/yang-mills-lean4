## Executive Summary
Novikov et al. (1983) and Witten (1982) analyzed 4D N=1 SYM, establishing the non-vanishing Witten index I_W = N, gluino condensation <lambda lambda> = Lambda^3 exp(2 pi i k / N), spontaneous breaking of discrete Z_2N chiral symmetry to Z_2, and the exact NSVZ beta function.

## Key Proven Results
- Exact calculation of the all-orders NSVZ beta function in N=1 supersymmetric gauge theories.
- Proof of non-zero vacuum expectation values <lambda lambda> != 0 and N degenerate discrete vacua.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
