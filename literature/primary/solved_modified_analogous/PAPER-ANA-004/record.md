- **MASS_OR_GAP_MECHANISM:** 3D semiclassical dilute monopole gas inducing magnetic Debye screening and linear Wilson loop area law
- **TRANSFER_BARRIER:** Relies on an adjoint Higgs field providing a classical symmetry-breaking scale v = <Phi> >> g^2 with point-like topological 't Hooft-Polyakov monopoles in 3D. In pure 4D YM, there is no scalar Higgs field, monopoles are 1D worldlines rather than 3D instanton points, and strong IR coupling destroys the dilute semiclassical approximation.
- **TRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Concept of magnetic charge disordering electric Wilson loops, generating dual Debye screening and linear confinement.
- **NONTRANSFERABLE_COMPONENT:** Adjoint Higgs scalar condensation, pointlike 3D monopole instantons, and weak-coupling semiclassical expansion v >> g^2.

## Executive Summary
Polyakov (1977) derived confinement in 3D compact U(1) / Georgi-Glashow model by summing a dilute gas of magnetic monopoles, showing that magnetic Debye screening generates a non-zero photon mass m_D ~ exp(-S_inst/2) and linear confinement at all couplings.

## Key Proven Results
- Semiclassical derivation of exponential correlation decay and mass gap in 3D gauge theory.
- Demonstration of Wilson area law from topological magnetic monopole condensation.

## Limitations and Unresolved Boundaries
- Semiclassical physical derivation, not a rigorous constructive mathematical theorem for 3D Georgi-Glashow.
- Requires scalar Higgs field and 3D spacetime; does not transfer directly to pure 4D Yang-Mills.
