## 3. Exact Mathematical Lemma Statement

### Lemma YM-011-L1 (UV Scale-Transfer and Observables Mollification Lemma)

Let $G = SU(N)$ with $N \ge 2$ in $d = 4$ spacetime dimensions.
Let $\Lambda = a \mathbb{Z}^4 \cap [-L_0, L_0]^4$ be a finite hypercubic lattice with spacing $a$ and volume $V = (2L_0)^4$.
Let $g_0 \le g_*$ be a bare coupling satisfying Bałaban's small-field contractivity threshold ($g_* \approx 0.28$).
Let $d\mu^{(k)}[A]$ be the $k$-th scale effective functional measure obtained after $k$ block-spin renormalization group transformations at physical block scale $L_k = a 2^k \le L_*$, where the effective coupling satisfies $g_k \le g_*$.

For any flow parameter $t > 0$, let $B_\mu(t, x)$ be the gauge field generated by the Wilson gradient flow equation on $\Lambda$:
$$\partial_t B_\mu(t, x) = - g_0^2 \, \frac{\delta S_{\text{Wilson}}[B]}{\delta B_\mu(t, x)}, \qquad B_\mu(0, x) = A_\mu(x)$$

Let $C$ be any rectifiable, non-self-intersecting closed loop of perimeter $|C|$ composed of lattice links, such that $|C| \ge L_k$.
Let $W(C) = \frac{1}{N} \mathrm{Tr} \, \mathcal{P} \exp(i \oint_C A)$ be the unflowed Wilson loop, and let $W_t(C) = \frac{1}{N} \mathrm{Tr} \, \mathcal{P} \exp(i \oint_C B(t))$ be the flowed Wilson loop.

**Statement:**
There exist strictly positive constants $C_0(N) < \infty$ and $t_* > 0$, depending only on $N$ and $g_*$, such that for all flow times $0 < t \le t_* L_k^2$:
$$\left| \langle W_t(C) \rangle^{(k)} - \langle W(C) \rangle^{(k)} \right| \le C_0(N) \, g_k^2 \left( \frac{t}{|C|^2} + \frac{a^2}{t} \right)$$
uniformly in the volume $L_0$ and the lattice spacing $a$.
