## 1. Executive Purpose & Epistemic Demarcation

The multi-scale hybrid strategy proposed in Tasks `YM-008` through `YM-010` combined six mathematical and computational techniques to address the 4D Yang–Mills problem. 

The emergency adversarial audit (`YM-GOV-011`) demonstrated that the previous candidate commits prematurely promoted this hybrid framework from a **candidate research strategy** to an **alleged mathematical proof of global closure and the mass gap**. Specifically:
1. Interface matching error bounds ($\mathcal{O}(a^2 |\ln a|)$ and $\mathcal{O}(e^{-c_3 \beta_0})$) were stipulated without proof.
2. Variational trial-state calculations in loop space were incorrectly inverted to assert a lower bound on the entire Hamiltonian spectrum.
3. Heuristic physical units ("fm" and "GeV") from QCD phenomenology were improperly hardcoded into formal mathematical proof hypotheses.
4. Reflection positivity (OS3) was asserted to hold automatically under gradient flow, ignoring infinite Gaussian heat-kernel tails across the reflection plane.

**The Repaired Stance:**
We do **not** discard the hybrid strategy. The conceptual insights—decomposing the problem into scale regimes where specific techniques possess natural leverage—remain valuable. However, all six components are stripped of false theorem claims and cataloged strictly as **candidate research directions**. All phenomenological units ("fm" and "GeV") are purged from load-bearing mathematical formulations.

## 2. Comprehensive 10-Field Evaluation Matrix for Candidate Research Directions

The six components of the repaired hybrid strategy are evaluated below across the exact 10 required mathematical and epistemic fields:

### Candidate 1: Constructive Renormalization Group (Bałaban Program)
1. **Exact Mathematical Object:** Multiscale block-spin effective actions $\mathcal{S}^{(k)}[A] = \frac{1}{2g_k^2} \| d_A A \|^2 + \mathcal{V}^{(k)}[A]$ generated by high-frequency Gaussian fluctuation integration with axial block gauge fixing.
2. **Known Theorem Supporting It:** Bałaban's UV Stability Theorem (1985–1989, `THM-YM-0018`): establishes contractivity $\|\mathcal{V}^{(k)}\|_E \le C g_k^2$ on localized polymer spaces for $g_k \le g_* \approx 0.28$.
3. **Unproved Hypothesis:** Extension of the effective action sequence to the infinite-volume thermodynamic limit $L_0 \to \infty$ and through the intermediate coupling regime $g_k \to 1$.
4. **Required Function Space:** Banach space of localized, gauge-covariant polynomial functionals endowed with exponential tree-decay polymer norms $\|\cdot\|_E$.
5. **Required Uniform Bound:** Uniform contractivity of fluctuation integrals: $\sup_{k} \|\mathcal{V}^{(k)}\|_E / g_k^2 < \infty$.
6. **Gauge Invariance:** Preserved under localized block gauge transformations; full continuum gauge invariance recovered asymptotically.
7. **Reflection Positivity:** Preserved at bare lattice level; requires careful verification across block-spin boundaries.
8. **Continuum Compatibility:** Fully compatible; explicitly designed to control the ultraviolet continuum scaling limit $a \to 0$.
9. **Spectral Relevance:** Provides control of the short-distance UV operator product expansion and Hamiltonian kinetic term.
10. **Potential Counterexample:** Gribov copy ambiguities in non-perturbative gauge orbits preventing unique background field decomposition as $g_k$ approaches 1.

### Candidate 2: Gradient Flow Regularization (Lüscher Flow)
1. **Exact Mathematical Object:** Parabolic diffusion equation on the gauge manifold: $\partial_t B_\mu(t, x) = D_\nu G_{\nu\mu}(t, x)$ with initial data $B_\mu(0, x) = A_\mu(x)$.
2. **Known Theorem Supporting It:** Lüscher's Flow Finiteness Theorem (2010, `THM-YM-0021`): gauge-invariant local composite operators constructed from flowed fields $B_\mu(t)$ require no additional ultraviolet renormalization for flow time $t > 0$.
3. **Unproved Hypothesis:** Existence of a non-trivial continuum functional measure under joint scaling $t(a) = c_0 a^2 \to 0$ that simultaneously eliminates contact singularities while strictly preserving reflection positivity.
4. **Required Function Space:** Sobolev spaces $H^s(\mathbb{R}^4, \mathfrak{su}(N))$ and smooth connections on Principal $G$-bundles.
5. **Required Uniform Bound:** Uniform bounds on heat kernel mollification errors: $\| B_\mu(t) - A_\mu \|_{H^{-1}} \le C \sqrt{t}$.
6. **Gauge Invariance:** Manifestly gauge-covariant under all spacetime-dependent gauge transformations $g(x) \in \mathcal{G}$.
7. **Reflection Positivity:** **`OS3_NOT_ESTABLISHED`**. The Gaussian heat kernel $K_t(x-y)$ has infinite tails and leaks across the $x_0 = 0$ reflection plane, mixing positive and negative Euclidean times for any finite $t > 0$.
8. **Continuum Compatibility:** Highly compatible; smoothens lattice fields into continuum-differentiable gauge fields.
9. **Spectral Relevance:** Provides UV-finite operator representatives for transfer matrix trial states.
10. **Potential Counterexample:** A non-zero diffusion tail across $x_0 = 0$ inducing negative-norm states in the physical Hilbert space completion.

### Candidate 3: Gauge-Invariant Loop Observables & Makeenko–Migdal Equations
1. **Exact Mathematical Object:** Wilson loop traces $W(C) = \frac{1}{N} \mathrm{Tr} \, \mathcal{P} \exp(i \oint_C A)$ on loop space $\mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^4)$, and the loop Laplacian $\Delta_C = \oint dx^\mu \oint dy^\mu \frac{\delta^2}{\delta \sigma_{\mu\nu}(x) \delta \sigma_{\mu\nu}(y)}$.
2. **Known Theorem Supporting It:** Makeenko & Migdal (1979, `THM-YM-0010`): exact Schwinger–Dyson equations on loop space; Kazakov & Kostov (1980) exact solvability in 2D.
3. **Unproved Hypothesis:** Rigorous functional-analytic definition of the loop delta function $\delta^{(4)}(x - y)$ and closure of the infinite Makeenko–Migdal hierarchy in 4D.
4. **Required Function Space:** Space of loops with bounded variation and Fréchet differentiable loop functionals.
5. **Required Uniform Bound:** A priori bound on loop correlators: $|W(C)| \le 1$, and Mandelstam inequality bounds on product loops.
6. **Gauge Invariance:** Manifest; Wilson loop traces are strictly invariant under all local gauge transformations.
7. **Reflection Positivity:** Holds on planar and cylindrical loops reflected across a cutting hyperplane.
8. **Continuum Compatibility:** Requires regularization of loop self-intersections and path-ordering divergences.
9. **Spectral Relevance:** Transfer matrix eigenvalues are directly encoded in the asymptotic decay of rectangular loops $W(R \times T) \sim e^{-E(R) T}$.
10. **Potential Counterexample:** Non-uniqueness of solutions to truncated loop equations without non-perturbative boundary conditions.

### Candidate 4: Semidefinite Programming (SDP) Loop Bootstrap
1. **Exact Mathematical Object:** Positive-semidefinite loop moment matrix $\mathcal{M}_{ij} = \langle \mathcal{O}_i^\dagger \mathcal{O}_j \rangle \succeq 0$ subject to linear Makeenko–Migdal constraints and Mandelstam quadratic inequalities.
2. **Known Theorem Supporting It:** Kazakov & Zheng (2023, 2024): rigorous numerical upper and lower bounds on finite-perimeter lattice Wilson loops via convex relaxation.
3. **Unproved Hypothesis:** Convergence of finite-perimeter truncated SDP bounds to the exact infinite-dimensional continuum theory as loop perimeter $L_{\max} \to \infty$ and $a \to 0$.
4. **Required Function Space:** Cone of positive-semidefinite matrices over infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert spaces of loop states.
5. **Required Uniform Bound:** Uniform non-emptiness of the feasible set and strict positivity of the spectral radius of the moment matrix.
6. **Gauge Invariance:** Strictly preserved; the moment matrix is constructed exclusively from gauge-invariant loop operators.
7. **Reflection Positivity:** Preserved if loop basis is restricted to positive-time reflected pairs.
8. **Continuum Compatibility:** Numerical bounds established at fixed $a$; continuum limit requires unproved scaling of loop constraints.
9. **Spectral Relevance:** Provides variational bounds on transfer matrix eigenvalues.
10. **Potential Counterexample:** A relaxed convex boundary that permits spurious, non-unitary measures satisfying truncated moments but violating full 4D unitarity.
