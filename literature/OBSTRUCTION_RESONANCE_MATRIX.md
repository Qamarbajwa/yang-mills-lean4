# YM-002F — Obstruction Resonance Matrix

Scores are categorical relations between a pathway and a candidate failure
locality. `DIRECT` means the source explicitly stops there; `STRONG` means the
source-bound limitation and the candidate statement have the same interface;
`PARTIAL` means only one component is shared. No score is a probability or a
proof of impossibility.

## Dimension notes

The matrix keeps the seven-dimensional resonance vocabulary used by ROS
(`THESIS`, `MECHANISM`, `ASSUMPTIONS`, `SCALING`, `BRIDGE_VARIABLE`,
`FAILURE_LOCALITY`, `OBSERVABLE`) conceptually separate from these obstruction
scores. In particular, a `DIRECT` score for `O17-SPECTRAL` on a propagator row
does not say the propagator is useless; it says the required gauge-invariant
Hamiltonian identification is absent from that record.
