## Classification discipline

Technical limitations of a method are recorded in the pathway matrix. Proven
obstructions would require a separate theorem and are absent here. The rows
above therefore remain candidates or recurring patterns, even when the same
interface appears in many regimes. Counterevidence and exceptions are kept so
the synthesis cannot silently harden into an impossibility claim.
