## 1. Epistemic Classification Taxonomy

To ensure mathematical integrity and prevent conflation between empirical test suites and mathematical proofs, all theorems are evaluated under the strict five-tier classification:
1. `CODE_VERIFIED`: Software implementation matches algebraic definitions and passes deterministic regression tests.
2. `ALGEBRAICALLY_VERIFIED`: Exact algebraic steps, identities, and cancellations verified by symbolic or manual calculus.
3. `SOURCE_SUPPORTED`: Primary published literature establishes the result under stated hypotheses.
4. `MATHEMATICALLY_PROVED`: Complete, closed mathematical proof established without external unverified gaps.
5. `INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED`: Audited and verified by blind E4 independent verification.
