## Reading boundaries

The A–D rows are not ranked by importance; they are the accepted theorem and
pathway records. E rows preserve the YM-002E audit outcomes. `CLAY_SUFFICIENT`
is `NO` for every row because the target requires one non-trivial continuum
theory for every compact simple group, OS/Wightman control, and a positive
Hamiltonian gap. A repeated `O*` code is a failure locality, not a theorem of
non-existence.
