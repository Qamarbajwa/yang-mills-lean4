# Verifiable reporting at every interval

Progress is a vector, not a scalar:
- tasks planned; tasks actually attempted; tasks that ended execution; tasks submitted; tasks independently accepted;
- proof steps proposed/formally checked (real tool), obligations open/closed, invalidated steps;
- independent pathways viable/refuted/blocked;
- formal theorem kernel verification under precise axioms (boolean only when trusted), semantic correspondence audit, independent acceptance;
- discoveries (verified scope), open conjectures, negative results, known counterexamples and all unreviewed sources;
- source/context/permission health and cost/latency measured facts.

Never present executed task fraction as theorem-completion percentage. A disproven step may be valuable research progress but is not progress toward verifying the false claim. Report model caveats specifically, not overgeneralized refusal. If a theorem has been formally verified under a specified axiom set, state that exact established fact even if publication acceptance remains pending (avoid underclaim). If no trusted Lean environment is observed, state `NOT_RUN` rather than pretending theorem disproved or verified.

A status cannot be elevated by an AI's own JSON, a copied stdout block, or another agent's agreement. Formal verification from an authentic, pinned runner under allowed axioms may merit `KERNEL_CHECKED_PENDING_SEMANTICS`, not `ORIGINAL_PROBLEM_SOLVED`.
