## 1. Governing Objective

\\boxed{\\textbf{Produce reliable, novel, explainable, reproducible, cumulative mathematical understanding}}

Mathematical research in this repository is not structured as an ad-hoc or ungrounded proof-search loop. Every research artifact, theorem candidate, adversarial counterexample, refutation, or limitation must advance mathematical knowledge monotonically without memory loss, while maintaining strictly reversible epistemic status upon discovery of counterexamples or scope boundaries.

## 2. The Seven Epistemic Layers

Research flows through seven distinct, non-conflated stages:

1. **Identity Layer:** Define the immutable mathematical problem contract with source-reconciled official requirements, formulation relations, and non-equivalent boundary targets.
2. **Knowledge Layer:** Maintain the verified semantic ledger of established theorems, mathematical definitions, proved limitations, and known obstructions.
3. **Discovery Layer:** Formulate hypotheses, generate micro-lemmas, identify physical/geometric mechanisms, and execute preliminary prior-art novelty screens.
4. **Falsification Layer:** Systematically attack every candidate lemma with adversarial counterexample families, degenerate parameter limits, dimension reductions, and semantic failure checks before proof investment.
5. **Verification Layer:** Establish correctness through multi-axis independent verification vectors and Lean/formalization checks, computing the critical load-bearing cut-set of the proof DAG.
6. **Understanding Layer:** Isolate the central load-bearing mechanism, minimize auxiliary assumptions, determine failure localities, and distill explainable canonical expositions.
7. **Evolution Layer:** Ingest verified results into structural memory, execute 7D vector pathway resonance, isolate candidate common obstructions, generalize theorems, and feed memory loops.

## 5. Candidate Identity & Deterministic Scientific Tree Hashing

To resolve the distinction between reviewed scientific content and post-review administrative bookkeeping:

$$\boxed{ \text{Accepted Scientific State Valid} \iff F_{\rm scientific}^{\rm audit} = F_{\rm scientific}^{\rm acceptance} }$$

where $F_{\rm scientific}$ is the deterministic SHA-256 tree hash of all theorem statements, proofs, relevant dependencies, source records, and pathway registers.

R3 discovery is part of candidate identity: the repository is classified before
the tree hash is computed. The classification policy is explicit and
integrity-bound; any `UNKNOWN` research-shaped artifact fails closed, and every
`SCIENTIFIC` artifact must be present in the canonical fingerprint manifest.

Lineage records:
- `AUDITED_CANDIDATE_SHA`: Git SHA audited by the independent E4 verifier.
- `ACCEPTANCE_COMMIT_SHA`: Git commit sealed upon contract acceptance.
- `SEALED_CHECKPOINT_SHA`: State captured in `checkpoints/`.
- `AUDITED_SCIENTIFIC_TREE_HASH`: Scientific tree hash at audit.
- `ACCEPTED_SCIENTIFIC_TREE_HASH`: Scientific tree hash at acceptance.
- `POST_E4_CHANGE_CLASS`:
  - `NONE`: No modifications post-E4.
  - `ADMINISTRATIVE_ONLY`: Only review receipts, test logs, task graph state, and contract ledger bookkeeping modified. (E4 remains valid).
  - `SCIENTIFIC_MATERIAL`: Any scientific theorem, definition, proof, or source record modified. ($\boxed{\text{E4 = STALE}}$).

## 9. Constitutional Amendment Protocol

ROS v1.0 is a **PROTECTED_VERSIONED_CONSTITUTION**. It cannot be silently mutated. Any amendment requires:
1. CHANGE_PROPOSAL: Explicit motivation and diff specification.
2. RATIONALE: Mathematical, empirical, or operational justification.
3. IMPACT_ANALYSIS: Full audit of existing theorems, pathways, and contracts.
4. REGRESSION_TESTS: Automated test suite covering new invariants.
5. NEW_VERSION: Monotonic version bump (e.g. v1.1.0).
6. INDEPENDENT_REVIEW: Fresh blind E4 audit of the amendment packet.
7. HUMAN_OWNER_ACCEPTANCE: Explicit sign-off by human owner.
8. SEALED_MIGRATION_RECEIPT: Cryptographically sealed checkpoint commit.
