   Theorem `YM-015-W1` proves that the 2-point connected Schwinger function is strictly non-zero ($S_2^c > 0$), establishing non-trivial quantum fluctuations. However, a non-perturbative lower bound proving that the theory is non-Gaussian ($S_4^c \neq 0$) remains open downstream.
