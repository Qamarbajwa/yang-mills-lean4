### 2. Repaired Faddeev–Popov Tree-Gauge Functional Identity
- **First-Principles Derivation:**
  Let $T \subset \Lambda$ be a maximal spanning tree on the lattice, and let $G_0 = \{h \in G^\Lambda : h(x_0) = 1\}$ be the rooted site gauge group. Because a tree contains no closed loops, there are no Gribov copies on $T$, and for any link configuration $A$, there exists a unique gauge transformation $h \in G_0$ such that $A^h|_T = 0$.
  The Faddeev–Popov resolution of unity on a single configuration orbit is defined by:
  $$\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A) \int_{G_0} dh \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A^h(b)) = 1.$$
  Therefore, on any single configuration orbit, the group integral evaluates to the **scalar**:
  $$\int_{G_0} dh \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A^h(b)) = \frac{1}{\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A)}.$$
  Equating this orbit integral to a slice distribution $\prod_{b \in T} \delta(A(b))$ evaluated at an arbitrary initial configuration $A$ is a mathematical error. Setting the observable $\mathcal{O} = 1$ immediately demonstrates the defect:
  $$\frac{1}{\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A)} \ne \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A(b)).$$
  At any configuration where $A|_T \ne 0$, the slice distribution vanishes identically, whereas the orbit scalar integral is non-zero ($1/\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}} = 1$ for constant tree Jacobian).
- **Configuration-Space Functional Integral Resolution:**
  The gauge-slice delta distribution $\prod_{b \in T} \delta(A(b))$ arises strictly under the full configuration-space functional integral:
  $$\int d\mu(A) \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g A)] = \int d\mu(A) \left[ \Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A) \int_{G_0} dh \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A^h(b)) \right] \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g A)].$$
  Interchanging the order of integration via Fubini's theorem:
  $$= \int_{G_0} dh \int d\mu(A) \Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A) \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A^h(b)) \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g A)].$$
  Performing the functional change of variables $A \mapsto \tilde{A} = A^h$ (so $A = \tilde{A}^{h^{-1}}$):
  - Invariance of functional measure: $d\mu(\tilde{A}^{h^{-1}}) = d\mu(\tilde{A})$,
  - Invariance of tree determinant: $\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, \tilde{A}^{h^{-1}}) = \Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, \tilde{A})$,
  - Invariance of gauge observable: $\mathcal{O}[(V \exp(g \tilde{A}))^{h^{-1}}] = \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g \tilde{A})]$.
  The inner integral becomes completely independent of $h$:
  $$\int d\mu(\tilde{A}) \Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, \tilde{A}) \prod_{b \in T} \delta(\tilde{A}(b)) \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g \tilde{A})].$$
  Integrating over $h \in G_0$ with normalized Haar measure ($\operatorname{Vol}(G_0) = 1$) yields:
  $$= \int d\mu(A) \Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}(V, A) \prod_{b \in T} \delta(A(b)) \mathcal{O}[V \exp(g A)].$$
  Here, and strictly here, the delta distribution $\prod_{b \in T} \delta(A(b))$ appears inside the functional integral $d\mu(A)$.

### 3. Site Gauge Transformations vs. Link Endpoint Actions
- **Endpoint Transformation Law:**
  For link variable $U_{xy} = V_{xy} \exp(g A_{xy})$ connecting site $x$ to site $y$, a site gauge transformation $h = (h_x)_{x \in \Lambda}$ acts at both endpoints:
  $$U_{xy}^h = h_x U_{xy} h_y^{-1}.$$
- **Failure of Fixed-Background Fluctuation Action:**
  If background $V_{xy}$ is held fixed while fluctuation $A_{xy}$ is transformed by an adjoint action at one endpoint ($A_{xy} \mapsto h_y A_{xy} h_y^{-1}$), the composite link becomes:
  $$U'_{xy} = V_{xy} \exp(g h_y A_{xy} h_y^{-1}) = V_{xy} h_y \exp(g A_{xy}) h_y^{-1}.$$
  Comparing $U'_{xy}$ to $U_{xy}^h$:
  $$h_x V_{xy} \exp(g A_{xy}) h_y^{-1} \stackrel{?}{=} V_{xy} h_y \exp(g A_{xy}) h_y^{-1} \iff h_x V_{xy} = V_{xy} h_y \iff h_x = V_{xy} h_y V_{xy}^{-1}.$$
  This holds if and only if $h$ is covariantly constant with respect to the background connection $V$. For generic site gauge transformations, $h_x \ne V_{xy} h_y V_{xy}^{-1}$.
- **Bałaban's Compensating Transformations:**
  In Bałaban R-I (CMP 122, 175–202, eqs. (1.85)–(1.88)), compensating covariance requires BOTH background $V$ and fluctuation $A$ to transform jointly:
  $$V_{xy} \mapsto V_{xy}^h = h_x V_{xy} h_y^{-1}, \quad A_{xy} \mapsto (h^{-1} A h)_{xy} = h_y A_{xy} h_y^{-1}.$$
  Then:
  $$V_{xy}^h \exp(g (h^{-1} A h)_{xy}) = (h_x V_{xy} h_y^{-1}) (h_y \exp(g A_{xy}) h_y^{-1}) = h_x V_{xy} \exp(g A_{xy}) h_y^{-1} = U_{xy}^h.$$

  Asserting an uncoupled tree-gauge identity on fluctuation $A$ with fixed background $V$ is mathematically defective. The actual-model Bałaban coordinate pullback and tree-gauge orbit identity across all external bonds $B_0$, charts, and cutoffs are properly classified as **CONDITIONAL_AND_OPEN** (`ACTUAL_BALABAN_COORDINATE_PULLBACK_AND_ORBIT_IDENTITY_OPEN`).
