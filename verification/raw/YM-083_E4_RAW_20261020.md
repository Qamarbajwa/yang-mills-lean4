     Concentrating all transverse plaquettes onto block boundaries scales interaction strength by $L^{D-2}$. Chessboard reflection positivity and Jensen's inequality yield:
     $$\ln Z \le \ln Z_{\mathrm{high}}(\beta) = \frac{1}{L^D} \ln Z\left( L^{D-2} \beta \right).$$
