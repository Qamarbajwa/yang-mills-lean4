## 2. SCHUR ORTHOGONALITY & 2D HAAR CHARACTER DECIMATION

### 2.1 Group-Theoretic Derivation
Let $G = SU(N)$ and let $d\mu(U)$ denote the normalized Haar measure on $G$. Let $D^r(U)$ denote irreducible representation matrices of dimension $d_r$. The character is $\chi_r(U) = \operatorname{Tr} D^r(U) = \sum_{i=1}^{d_r} D^r_{ii}(U)$.

By the Peter–Weyl theorem:
$$\int_{SU(N)} d\mu(U) D^r_{ij}(U) [D^{r'}_{kl}(U)]^* = \frac{1}{d_r} \delta_{r r'} \delta_{ik} \delta_{jl}.$$
Since representation matrices are unitary, $[D^{r'}_{kl}(U)]^* = D^{r'}_{lk}(U^{-1})$. For two matrices $V_1, V_2 \in SU(N)$:
$$\int_{SU(N)} d\mu(U) D^r_{ai}(U) D^{r'}_{kb}(U^{-1}) = \frac{\delta_{rr'}}{d_r} \delta_{ab} \delta_{ik}.$$
Multiplying by $D^r_{ia}(V_1)$ and $D^{r'}_{bk}(V_2)$ and contracting all indices:
$$\sum_{i, a, k, b} D^r_{ia}(V_1) D^{r'}_{bk}(V_2) \frac{\delta_{rr'}}{d_r} \delta_{ab} \delta_{ik} = \frac{\delta_{rr'}}{d_r} \sum_{i, a} D^r_{ia}(V_1) D^r_{ai}(V_2) = \frac{\delta_{rr'}}{d_r} \operatorname{Tr}[D^r(V_1 V_2)] = \frac{\delta_{rr'}}{d_r} \chi_r(V_1 V_2).$$
Hence:
$$\int_{SU(N)} d\mu(U) \chi_r(V_1 U) \chi_{r'}(U^{-1} V_2) = \frac{\delta_{r r'}}{d_r} \chi_r(V_1 V_2).$$

### 2.2 Application to Plaquette Decimation
Expanding the single-plaquette Boltzmann weight in characters:
$$e^{-S(p)} = c_0 \left[ 1 + \sum_{r \ne 0} d_r a_r \chi_r(U(p)) \right], \quad a_r = \frac{c_r}{d_r c_0}.$$
When integrating an internal link $U$ shared by two adjacent plaquettes $p_1, p_2$ with orientations $V_1 U$ and $U^{-1} V_2$:
$$\int_{SU(N)} d\mu(U) \, d_r a_r \chi_r(V_1 U) \cdot d_r a_r \chi_r(U^{-1} V_2) = d_r^2 a_r^2 \cdot \frac{1}{d_r} \chi_r(V_1 V_2) = d_r a_r^2 \chi_r(V_1 V_2).$$
The character activity of the combined double plaquette $p_1 \cup p_2$ is:
