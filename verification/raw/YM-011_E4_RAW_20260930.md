     $$\Sigma_{\mathrm{UV}} = \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} 2^{-j} \frac{g_j^2}{g_0^2} \le \frac{1}{1 - 1/2} = 2.$$

     $$\sum_{j=0}^{k-1} 2^{-j} g_j^2 \le 2 g_0^2 \sum_{j=0}^\infty 2^{-j} = 4 g_0^2.$$

   - **Impact:** Minor constant prefactor mismatch (2 vs 4) between statement and proof/code. Does not affect geometric convergence or $O(g_0^2 |C|/a)$ scaling.
