# SOURCE OF TRUTH

Priority order for scientific claims:

1. Official Clay/Jaffe–Witten problem specification.
2. Primary published mathematical papers.
3. Authoritative corrections/errata.
4. Independently reproduced derivations.
5. Secondary reviews.
6. Numerical experiments.
7. Model-generated summaries.

A lower-priority source may not silently override a higher-priority source.

Every imported claim must identify whether it is:
`FACT | THEOREM | NUMERICAL_RESULT | INFERENCE | HYPOTHESIS | ASSUMPTION | UNVERIFIED_CLAIM`.
